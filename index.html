Quiet Sound — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 November 2026

Quiet Sound is a calm-sounds app for focus, rest and sleep, made by an independent developer (Bilel, Tunisia). This policy explains what the app collects, why, and how you stay in control. Questions: lightaura625@gmail.com.

What the app collects

  • Your account (only if you sign in). If you choose “Continue with Google”, we receive your name, email address and profile photo from your Google account.
  • Your content and settings. Your saved sound mixes, your in-app moons, and your preferences are stored in Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore), tied to your account, so they come back when you sign in on another phone.
  • Advertising (free plan only). On the free plan, Google AdMob may collect your advertising ID, IP address, device identifiers, and ad-interaction data to show and measure ads. In Europe and the UK we ask for your consent before ads load.
  • Diagnostics. Basic, non-identifying crash and performance data to keep the app working.

The app does not collect your location, contacts, calendar, microphone, gallery photos, health or financial information.

Why we use it

  • To run the app and play sounds.
  • To save your mixes, moons and settings and sync them across your devices.
  • To show and measure ads on the free plan (Premium removes ads).
  • To understand crashes and keep the app stable.

Who it is shared with

Your data is processed by Google as our service providers — Google Sign-In and Firebase (accounts and your saved content) and Google AdMob (ads). We do not sell your data, and we do not share it with anyone else for their own marketing.

Premium subscriptions

Premium is billed by Google Play. Quiet Sound never sees your card or payment details — Google Play handles the whole transaction. Manage or cancel a subscription in the Google Play app (Payments & subscriptions).

Your choices and deleting your data

You can delete your account and everything tied to it at any time: in the app, open Profile → Delete my account. You can also ask by email. Full steps are on the account-deletion page.

Children

Quiet Sound is not directed to children under 13 (under 16 in the EEA). We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Security

Data sent between the app and Google’s services is encrypted in transit (HTTPS).

Changes

If this policy changes, the “last updated” date above changes with it. Material changes will be reflected in the app’s store listing.
