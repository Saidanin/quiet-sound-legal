Delete your Quiet Sound account

How to remove your account and all data tied to it.

Quiet Sound lets you delete your account and everything connected to it, from inside the app or by email. This is permanent and cannot be undone.

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open Quiet Sound.
  2. Tap the profile icon in the top-right.
  3. Scroll down and tap Delete my account.
  4. Confirm. Your account and data are removed right away, and you are signed out.

By email

If you can’t open the app, email lightaura625@gmail.com from the email address you signed in with, with the subject “Delete my Quiet Sound account”. We will delete your account within 30 days and confirm by reply.

What is deleted

  • Your account record (your Google-linked sign-in for Quiet Sound).
  • Your saved sound mixes, your moons, and your app settings stored on our servers (the users/<your id> document in Firebase).

Anonymous, aggregated statistics that are not linked to you may remain.

About Premium

A Premium subscription is billed and managed by Google Play, not by Quiet Sound. Deleting your account here does not cancel a subscription. To stop billing, cancel it in the Google Play app under Payments & subscriptions.
